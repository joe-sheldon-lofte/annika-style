Meet Annika May

Coastal Sophistication Meets Modern Technique

Hi, I’m Annika May. Growing up along Florida’s Treasure Coast, I’ve always held an appreciation for light, effortlessly chic aesthetics that hold up to our sun-drenched lifestyle.

I combine modern color techniques with disciplined foundation cutting. Whether you are looking for a seamless balayage refresh or a complete style elevation, my goal is to give you healthy, manageable hair that radiates confidence.
