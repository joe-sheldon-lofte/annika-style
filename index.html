Meet Annika May

Creating Hair That Makes You Feel Beautiful

Hi, I’m Annika May! Creativity and hands-on crafting have always felt second nature to me, and turning that natural passion into hair styling has been one of the most rewarding journeys of my life. I believe God gave me hands for this craft, and my ultimate goal is simple: when you look in the mirror after sitting in my chair, you leave feeling truly confident and radiant.

I specialize in highlighting and high-dimensional blonding techniques, currently refining my skills at the Beauty & Massage Institute of Vero Beach while interning at Stylush by CK.

When I’m not at school or in the salon, you’ll find me out on the ocean paddleboarding (keeping an eye out for my favorite marine animals—sharks!), picking up new sports with my friends, or soaking up everything our coastal Vero Beach community has to offer.
